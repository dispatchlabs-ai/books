# Governance

Books uses a lightweight maintainer-led model.

The Dispatch Labs maintainers listed in `CODEOWNERS` have final authority over
scope, accounting behavior, compatibility, security response, merges, and
releases. Maintainers may delegate review of a subsystem but retain final
responsibility for accepted changes.

The project is experimental and provides no guaranteed support or release
cadence. Decisions should favor accounting correctness, explicit boundaries,
small understandable changes, reproducible evidence, and long-term
maintainability over contribution volume.
